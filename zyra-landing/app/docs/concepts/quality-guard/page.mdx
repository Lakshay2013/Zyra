# Quality Guard

Cheaper models are only useful if responses stay correct for your users. **Quality Guard** is Zyra’s layer that decides when a downgrade is safe—and when to keep or upgrade tier.

## Principles

- **No silent surprises** — if a task looks like multi-step reasoning, tool-heavy workflow, or strict compliance text, Zyra biases toward capable models.
- **Equivalence-minded downgrades** — for rote transformations, extraction, or short Q&A, the system may route to smaller/faster models when signals align.
- **Feedback loop** — production traffic and outcomes inform future routing; the proxy is designed to improve over static “model X only” setups.

## Interaction with the cost engine

The cost engine proposes candidates; Quality Guard applies **safety and quality constraints** before a final choice is locked in. That means optimization is always bounded by an acceptable quality bar rather than “cheapest always wins.”

## Failure and fallback

If a provider errors, rate-limits, or returns an invalid payload, Zyra can fail over per your configured chain. Quality Guard still applies on the replacement hop so you do not accidentally cascade into an ill-suited model under stress.

## Operational tips

- Watch the dashboard for sudden shifts in model tier—often a sign of prompt or traffic mix changes.
- Keep system prompts explicit; ambiguous instructions increase conservative routing (higher cost, higher quality).
